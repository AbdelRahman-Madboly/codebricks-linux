# Exercise 1: Navigate and Read
Difficulty: Easy
Concepts: pwd, ls, cd, cat, head

## Scenario
You just opened a terminal and need to orient yourself, then read the first few lines of a file somewhere in your home directory.

## Your Task
1. Print your current working directory.
2. Navigate to your home directory using the `~` shortcut.
3. List the contents of your home directory.
4. Navigate into any subdirectory that exists there.
5. Find a text file inside it (any `.txt`, `.md`, or `.log` file) and print its first 5 lines.

## Check Your Work
The output of step 5 shows exactly 5 lines. The prompt shows you are inside the subdirectory, not at home.
